# Third-Party Notices

ViewerAtlas builds its interface directly on React and React Router. The map and
charts are drawn on canvas without a charting library, and no vendored
third-party component source is included in this repository.

The site uses the Source Serif 4 and IBM Plex Mono typefaces, both under the SIL
Open Font License 1.1, served by Google Fonts. The map's colours follow
matplotlib's tab10 and tab20b palettes.

Third-party dependencies and their licenses are listed in `package-lock.json`.
